# Code of Conduct

Oriel TV is built by people with different backgrounds, skills, and opinions. This code describes how we treat each other in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, and events.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Critique work, not people. Explain the reason behind feedback and suggest an alternative where you can.
- Welcome newcomers. Answer questions patiently, including ones that seem basic.
- Respect people's time. Keep discussions on topic and say clearly what you need.
- Accept decisions gracefully, and reopen them with new information rather than repetition.
- Give credit for other people's ideas and work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background, or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to do these things.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or continuing an interaction after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the project maintainer, [@vishwavinoth](https://github.com/vishwavinoth), privately. Include links or screenshots where you can. Reports are handled confidentially, and nobody will face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

A dedicated contact address will be added here before the repository becomes public.

## What happens next

The maintainer will review the report, talk to the people involved where appropriate, and respond in proportion to the harm. Responses range from a private note, to a public warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies in all Oriel TV project spaces, and wherever someone represents the project in public.
