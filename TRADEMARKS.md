# Oriel TV name and logo

The Oriel TV source code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. That license covers the code, not the project's name or logo (see section 6 of the license). This page explains how you may use them.

The name isn't a registered trademark yet. This policy states how the project asks people to use it, and it will be updated if the name is registered.

## You're welcome to

- Refer to Oriel TV by name in articles, videos, reviews, talks, and tutorials.
- Say that your product or fork is "based on Oriel TV" or "compatible with Oriel TV", as long as that's true.
- Use the unmodified logo to link to the official project.

## Please don't

- Name a fork, app, website, or company "Oriel" or "Oriel TV", or use a confusingly similar name.
- Publish a modified build under the Oriel name or logo. Rename your build and use your own icon.
- Suggest that the project endorses or supports your product when it doesn't.
- Change the logo's shape or colors, or combine it with other marks.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask.
